## What stays on your phone
Photos you take, the scans and PDFs made from them, and their titles are stored **only on your phone** by default,
in encrypted storage. We cannot see them.

## Camera
The app uses the camera only while you are scanning. Images are not sent anywhere unless you turn on backup.

## Account (optional)
You can use the app without an account. If you create one, we store your email address and display name with
Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in.

## Encrypted backup (optional, off by default)
If you turn on backup, copies of your documents are encrypted **on your phone** with a key protected by a
passphrase only you know, then stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). We and Google can see
the encrypted data's size and timestamps, but not its content. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered.

## Subscriptions
Payments are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase token and the subscription status to unlock features; we
never see your card details.

## Service providers
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check), operated by Google LLC
- Google Play Billing and Play Integrity, operated by Google LLC

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Deleting your data
- **In the app:** Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and all cloud backup data.
- **Without the app:** email [address] from your account's email with the subject "Delete my account", or use
  [web form URL]. We delete the account and all backup data within [30] days and confirm by email.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
